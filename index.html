About Me

I'm a senior at Belmont High School with a passion for computer engineering, robotics, computer science and physics. I am the programming captain of the KwarQs, a FRC robotics team, taking on a leadership position and aiding other students learn through STEM. I was an assistant researcher at a internship in Northeastern University's Young Scholars' Program, researching dark matter and using my Python coding skills to develop a funciton for astrophysicists to map dark matter.

I actively participate in Varsity skiing and play soccer, which have strengthened my teamwork, discipline, and perseverance while balancing athletics with academics. I am also involved in the Muslim Student Association, where I help foster community and promote cultural awareness within my school. In addition, I serve as a teacher assistant at the Islamic Center of Boston in Wayland, supporting younger students in their religious education and helping create a positive learning environment. I support other students as a physics tutor, helping them learn and develop there scientific knowledge.
